I am an Applied Scientist at [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/ai-for-good-research-lab/), working at the intersection of multimodal AI, agentic systems, computer vision, and real-world AI applications.

My work focuses on building intelligent systems that go beyond text interfaces — connecting foundation models with visual perception, knowledge retrieval, and real-time, in-person human interaction.

Recent work includes **Talk to TR**, a continuously operating conversational exhibit at the Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library built on a 300,000-record archival knowledge system; benchmarks and human studies for **deepfake and AI-generated media detection**; **vision-language models** for ecological and scientific imagery; and, during my graduate studies at the University of Miami, **3D segmentation of the human aorta** from CT images.

I am particularly interested in turning advances in foundation models into reliable, grounded systems that people use in the real world.
